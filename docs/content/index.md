# Iowa Design System

## Vision

One connected foundation. The Iowa Design System gives every Iowa experience a connected, consistent foundation — regardless of who creates it, where it appears, or what technology powers it.

## Mission

Built for everyone. Build and maintain the Iowa Design System — the shared design language, applied standards, reusable compositions, and technical guidance that give everyone who works with the Iowa brand what they need to produce quality, accessible work across every medium, tool, and platform.
