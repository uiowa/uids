# Color outline

Iowa's black and gold are among the most recognizable colors in higher education. Our color system builds on that foundation, defining roles for every color so they work together consistently across every format and medium.

## Brand

<hr>

Gold, black, and white are Iowa's primary colors and the foundation of the Iowa visual identity. These three colors should always dominate your work. Gold drives recognition in marketing and recruitment contexts, while black and white provide structure, contrast, and a more formal tone when the work calls for it.

## Data visualization

<hr>

Data colors are used for charts, graphs, and other visualizations where multiple categories need to be distinguished from each other. They are designed to be clear and accessible, but color should never be the only way data is differentiated. Always pair data colors with labels, patterns, or position.

## Semantic states

<hr>

Semantic colors communicate universal meaning across the system. They are used for alerts, messages, and system states, including info, success, warning, and danger. These colors should only appear in those contexts and should never be used decoratively. Each state has a soft message fill (with its edge color as the border) and a solid alert fill.
